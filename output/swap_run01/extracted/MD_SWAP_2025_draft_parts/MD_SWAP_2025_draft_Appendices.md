Southern wild rice Potomac River population affected by blue catfish invasion. Do not have a lot of data on this species, possibly due to sampling issues (its habitat straddles fresh and tidal). Have some recent records in iNaturalist in MD, though not many. Likely more abundant than records show, given its small size and SAV habitat (i.e., it can be hard to survey for). Historic species with a few recent records from Central/Southern MD.
