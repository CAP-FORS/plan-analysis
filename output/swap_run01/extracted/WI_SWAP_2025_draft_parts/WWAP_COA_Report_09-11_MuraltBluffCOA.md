The COA and the Monroe-Muralt Prairie Land Legacy area lie among rolling hills created by an old glacial moraine. The COA includes high quality prairie remnants on the rocky hilltops and slopes that are not �llable, and a small number of prairie pastures and oak savannas. Wet-mesic prairies exist along the valley floors and many rare prairie plants and insects occur in pockets throughout the area.
Although small, Muralt Bluff Prairie and Oliver Prairie SNAs have high-quality dry prairie communi�es that contain numerous rare plants and animals. Abraham's Woods SNA features an old-growth stand of southern mesic forest, which is increasingly rare in Wisconsin. Albany wildlife area has an excellent sand prairie with rich prairie along bike trail. Includes remnants of various stages of oak savanna (Oak Opening through Oak Woodland) and supports rare birds. A ten-mile segment of the Sugar River State Trail has high density of glade mallow.
