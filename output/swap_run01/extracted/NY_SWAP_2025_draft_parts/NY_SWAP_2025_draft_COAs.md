Appendix 6 includes maps of known locations of terrestrial and aquatic species tracked by the New York Natural Heritage Program (NYNHP) and used in the 2025 New York State Wildlife Action Plan. Not all locations in the state have been surveyed, and the locations shown on the maps are not inclusive of all the potential locations of tracked species. Many locations have not been surveyed recently, and some data shown may be outdated. These maps are for informational purposes only and should not be used in decision making for planning or project development. Contact the New York State Department of Environmental Conservation or NYNHP for more information.
